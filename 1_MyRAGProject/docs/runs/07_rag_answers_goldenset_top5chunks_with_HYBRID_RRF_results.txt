question: What is a leverage and how can i change it?
answer: Leverage is a financial tool that allows a client to control a larger position in the market with a smaller amount of capital. It amplifies both potential gains and potential losses.

You can change the leverage within your Client Area of each trading account up to five (5) times. If you wish to change it more than five (5) times, you need to send a request to tradingdesk@hfm.com. Additionally, you can change the leverage on demo accounts as many times as you want. There are no restrictions on increasing leverage, but you can only decrease it under certain conditions: if the margin is zero or if the margin is greater than zero and the margin level exceeds 100% [Account opening agreement(1).txt#131][Account opening agreement(1).txt#132].
