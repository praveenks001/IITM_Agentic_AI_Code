Additionally, you can change the leverage on demo accounts as many times as you want. There are no restrictions on increasing the leverage, but you can only decrease it under certain conditions: if the margin is zero or if the margin is greater than zero and the margin level exceeds 100% [Account opening agreement(1).txt#131].
