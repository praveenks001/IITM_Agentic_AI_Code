Question ID: 7
Question: Will my account be archived any time?
Ideal Answer: A trading Account, that is not a cent trading account, shall be archived if the following occurs:(a) No financial and/or trading activity and no pending orders for a set period of 60 (sixty)calendar days. A Cent Trading Account shall be archived upon the occurrence of any of the following circumstances:a) No financial and/or trading activity (excluding pending orders) has been conducted on the Cent Trading Account for a continuous period of fifteen (15) calendar days and the Account Balance is less than 100 USc. b) No financial and/or trading activity (excluding pending orders) has been conducted on the Cent Trading Account for a continuous period of thirty (30) calendar days, and the Account Balance is equal to or greater than 100 USc.
c) Upon the occurrence of either of the above, the Cent Trading Account shall be archived,and any pending orders shall be automatically deleted.Remaining balance in the Trading Account which will be Archived, shall be transferred to the wallet balance before the Trading Account is archived.Any Trading Account can be classified by the Company as an Archived, regardless of the remaining balance of the Trading Account. An Archived Account is not considered as a terminated trading account, however, any trading account may only be restored at the Company’s discretion.
