Q: A Copy Trading follower has 400 USD equity, the Strategy Provider has 5000 USD equity and the follower selects 100 percent Volume Allocation. What is the Copy Ratio?

A: The Copy Ratio can be calculated using the formula:

Copy Ratio = (Follower Equity / Strategy Provider Equity) x (Follower Volume Allocation / 100)

In this case:

- Follower Equity = 400 USD
- Strategy Provider Equity = 5000 USD
- Follower Volume Allocation = 100%

Plugging in the values:

Copy Ratio = (400 / 5000) x (100 / 100) = 0.08

Therefore, the Copy Ratio is 0.08.

Question 69/77
Q: A Strategy Provider has 5000 USD equity. A follower has 400 USD equity and selects 50 percent Volume Allocation. The Strategy Provider opens 0.10 lot. What is the theoretical follower position and what actually happens?

A: The theoretical follower position size can be calculated as follows:

1. Calculate the copy ratio:
   \[
   \text{Copy Ratio} = \frac{\text{Follower Equity}}{\text{Strategy Provider Equity}} \times \text{Volume Allocation}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Copy Ratio} = \frac{400}{5000} \times 0.50 = 0.04
   \]

2. Calculate the follower position size:
   \[
   \text{Follower Position Size} = \text{Strategy Provider Position Size} \times \text{Copy Ratio}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Follower Position Size} = 0.10 \times 0.04 = 0.004 \text{ lot}
   \]

Therefore, the theoretical follower position size is 0.004 lot. 

What actually happens is that the follower's trade will open with this calculated position size of 0.004 lot, which is the minimum trade size based on the calculations provided. [Copy trading TC(1).txt#123]
